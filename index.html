About EduHub

EduHub exists to make finding the right academic help simple. We sit between students and parents on one side, and tutors and academic experts on the other, and handle the introduction — no accounts, no apps to install, just a conversation.

Our mission is to make quality tuition and academic support easy to reach, for every subject and every level from school to university.

Who we help

  • Students looking for a subject tutor or exam prep
  • Parents arranging tuition for their children
  • Tutors who want more students
  • Academic experts offering project & assignment support
